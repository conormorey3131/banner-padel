What is padel?

Padel is the world's fastest-growing sport — a mix of tennis and squash, always played in doubles on an enclosed glass court.

  • Easy to learn. Underarm serves and a smaller court mean long, fun rallies from day one.
  • Seriously social. Always 2v2 — it's a game built around playing with friends.
  • Great exercise. Fast, fun and addictive — a workout that never feels like one.
  • For everyone. Kids, beginners, retired tennis players, competitive athletes — padel meets you at your level.

FAQ

Good questions

When does Banner Padel open?+

We're putting the finishing touches to the club now. Join the waiting list and you'll be the first to hear the opening date — plus get access to exclusive pre-launch offers.

I've never played padel — can I just turn up?+

Absolutely. Padel is one of the easiest racket sports to pick up, and our coaches and beginner sessions will have you rallying in your very first visit.

Do I need my own racket?+

No — rackets and balls will be available at the club, so you can try the game before investing in your own gear.

How many people play at once?+

Padel is played in doubles — two teams of two on an enclosed court. Come with three friends, or join one of our social sessions and we'll match you up.

Where is the club?+

Banner Padel is located in Ennis, Co. Clare — the first dedicated indoor padel facility in the county. Full address and directions coming soon.
